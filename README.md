# 💖 Romantic Birthday Wishes Experience For Your Girlfriend

A modern, thoughtful, and magical birthday website designed to wow her with romantic aesthetics, animations, and interactive surprises.

---

## ✨ Features Included

1. **Magical Floating Petals & Hearts Background**:
   - 60 FPS smooth canvas animation of falling rose petals and fairy light glows.
   - Interactive screen clicks that shoot floating glowing hearts and sparkles.
   - Heart Counter at the bottom tracking every beat of love.

2. **Ambient Music Box Synthesizer (`audio.js`)**:
   - Built-in soothing romantic piano / music-box arpeggio using the **Web Audio API**.
   - Zero external audio file dependencies — plays immediately on mobile or desktop without broken links.
   - Magical sound effects when blowing candles, breaking wax seals, and tapping hearts.

3. **Interactive 3D Birthday Cake & Candles**:
   - Realistic tiered cake with flickering animated candle flames.
   - Tap "Make a Wish & Blow Candles" to blow out the flames with smoke puffs.
   - Triggers multi-stage celebratory confetti fanfare and reveals a heartfelt birthday wish message.

4. **Vintage Sealed Romantic Love Letter**:
   - 3D envelope with a crimson wax seal.
   - Tap the wax seal to break it open, unfold the letterhead paper, and read your personal love letter.
   - Tap "Fold Back" to tuck it back into the envelope.

5. **"Our Memory Lane" Polaroid Gallery with 3D Flip**:
   - Authentic Polaroid photo cards with washi tape and playful rotations.
   - **Click any photo to flip it 180°** and reveal sweet handwritten secret notes on the back!
   - Built-in "+ Add Our Own Photo" button to easily add your real couple pictures directly from your device.

6. **Reasons Why I Love You & Quote Dispenser**:
   - Categorized quotes: *Deep Romantic*, *Cute & Playful*, *Sweet Little Things*, and *Our Future & Dreams*.
   - Tap "Draw Another Reason 💖" to cycle through heartfelt reminders.
   - **"Open When..." Virtual Notes**:
     - *Open When You Miss Me* 🥺
     - *Open When You Need a Laugh* 😂
     - *Open When You Can't Sleep* 🌙
     - *Open When You Feel Insecure* 👑

7. **"How Well Do You Know Us?" Romantic Mini-Quiz**:
   - Sweet couple questions with affectionate responses and progress tracking.

8. **Mystery Birthday Gift Box**:
   - 3D gift box with ribbon that unwraps on click.
   - Confetti explosion revealing an official VIP Romantic Coupon (*Unlimited Free Cuddles, Food Dates & Back Massages*).

9. **Lock Page / Logout Button 🔒**:
   - Tap the **"Lock Page 🔒"** button in the top navigation at any time.
   - Instantly returns back to the global countdown lock screen with live clock and floating nickname clouds.
   - Requires secret passcode **`2627`** or midnight countdown arrival to unlock again.
